About Sonali Resort

Sonali Resort is a hospitality destination in Los Baños, Laguna designed for guests looking for relaxation, recreation, group bonding, celebrations, and memorable stays.

Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers about the sample staycation concept and planning an inquiry.

What is the Relax & Reconnect package?

It is a proposed academic staycation concept for two, illustrated as a 2 days / 1 night getaway with accommodation, pool access, recreation, and leisure time. Confirm actual inclusions directly with the establishment.

Who is the package for?

The featured sample is designed for couples and two-person getaways. Sample packages for friends and families are also presented on this page.

How long is the stay?

The sample package duration is 2 days and 1 night. Actual check-in and check-out schedules must be confirmed with the resort.

Does the package include transportation?

No. Transportation is listed as an exclusion in this proposed academic package. Ask the resort about current arrangements and travel directions.

Can families and groups stay at the resort?

The website presents family and group-friendly sample stays. Please verify room capacity, facilities, and current guest policies before booking.

Can the resort be used for events?

An event room is described among the facilities. Contact the establishment to confirm event availability, capacity, setup, and any applicable fees.

How can I make an inquiry?

Use the Plan Your Stay form or contact the resort using the details listed below. The on-page form is a demonstration and does not send your message to the resort.

Should I confirm the rate before booking?

Yes. ₱4,999 is a proposed academic package rate, not an official published rate. Always confirm current rates, inclusions, availability, and booking terms with the establishment.
